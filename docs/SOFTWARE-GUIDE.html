How to use this document

Train staff in order: login → receive stock → price → sell → recover → close day. The owner keeps §Backups and §Users private.

1. Sign in and leave the desk

Open your company HTTPS address in Chrome or Safari. Use the shared mill login, then select your name and enter your four-digit PIN. Press Leave the desk before another person uses the PC.

3. Daily workflow (checklist)

  1. Add or verify products, buyers, and mills.
  2. Keeper receives cloth with lot number and quantity → waiting for rate.
  3. Bookkeeper prices received cloth or posts a priced purchase.
  4. Salesman creates a bill against a specific lot (credit = registered buyer; walk-in = paid in full).
  5. Record recovery from buyers and payments to mills.
  6. Use direct deals when cloth goes mill → buyer without entering the godown.
  7. Process returns only from the original bill or purchase.
  8. Review evening close, P&L, trial balance; share Excel note.
  9. Owner downloads encrypted backup.

5. Inventory rules

  • Every sale line uses a specific lot; the system prevents overselling.
  • Unpriced receipts stay in waiting for rate until the bookkeeper posts rates.
  • Posted sales automatically reduce stock — do not dispatch twice.
  • Direct deals never appear in godown movement history.

6. Recovery and cheques

  • Recovery applies to the oldest overdue invoice first.
  • PDC cheques are not cleared cash until deposited and presented.

7. Evening Excel (11 tabs)

Summary, Purchases, Sales, Recovery, Mill Payments, Stock, Returns, Waiting for Rate, Buyer Dues, Accounts, Evening Note. Pakistan dates. Owner/bookkeeper only for the full workbook.

8. Working on multiple computers

Same database for everyone. Lists refresh about every 5 seconds. If the internet drops, reconnect and check whether a bill posted before entering it again.

9. Backups and support

  • Owner: encrypted backup; key stored separately.
  • Agree opening balances before mixing old manual figures with live trade.
  • Support: bill number, screen, Pakistan time, error text — no passwords in chat.
