Godown Ledger

Textile trading · Lahore office

Open the company's HTTPS address. Each computer uses the same mill login, then the person sitting there enters their own PIN. Press Leave the desk before someone else takes over.

Who sees what

PersonWork

SalesmanBills, buyers and recovery. No books.

OwnerEverything, including People and pins and backups.

The daily book

  1. Add mills, buyers and products. The owner/bookkeeper approves opening balances.
  2. The keeper records cloth received with lot number and fractional quantity. The bookkeeper prices and posts it before it can be sold.
  3. Bill the chosen lot. Credit bills need a registered buyer; walk-in bills must be paid in full.
  4. Record recovery and mill payments. A post-dated cheque is not cleared cash.
  5. Use Direct deals when cloth goes straight from mill to buyer. Stock is unchanged.
  6. Make returns from their original bill or purchase. The server checks quantity, price and cost.
  7. Review Evening note, P&L and Trial balance. Share the note through WhatsApp/email and press Send. Automatic delivery requires a configured provider and schedule.
  8. The owner downloads an encrypted backup and keeps the key separately.

Working on another computer

All desks use one server database. Read screens refresh about every five seconds while visible. Updates pause during unfinished entries; finish or reopen that screen to refresh. If two tills want the last stock, the server refuses an oversell. Cloud trading needs Internet access; private financial pages are not cached for offline viewing.

Access and support

The owner adds people under More → People and pins. Each PIN is different. Five wrong attempts lock that PIN for 15 minutes. The owner can reset or disable a person. Never share the owner's PIN.

Historical manual books have not been imported. Agree the launch date and starting balances before entering live trade. Contact the administrator with the bill number, screen and Pakistan time of an error. Do not send secrets or decrypted backups in chat.
